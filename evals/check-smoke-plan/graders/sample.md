Judge the meaning, not the exact words. PASS if the plan uses a small sample (about 30 tickets)
that covers every team, has some way to get hard tickets into it (it names some, lets the person
force them in, or asks for them), and removes personal data (names, emails, phone numbers,
addresses) before anything is sent. FAIL if the sample, the hard tickets or the redaction is
missing.
